### Summary (177 characters)

```text
Control lights, shutters, thermostats, speakers and more in Home Assistant from your Ulanzi Deck. Keys show live states with HA icons; the dials react instantly to every detent.
```

### Detailed introduction (968 characters)

```text
HA Deck brings Home Assistant to the keys and dials of your Ulanzi Deck.

• HA Toggle – lights, switches, fans, covers, media players, thermostats with live state
• HA Dial – brightness, colour temp, volume, temperature, position, fan speed; press action configurable
• HA Dynamic Dial – hold a toggle key and one dial controls that entity
• HA Trigger – scenes, scripts, automations
• HA Sensor – values with unit, orange outside your limits
• HA Service Call – any service with JSON data

Keys show the same icons as Home Assistant, lights in their real colour. The display reacts to every dial detent instantly; the plugin reconnects automatically.

Setup: create a long-lived access token in your HA profile, drag an HA action onto a key, enter URL and token, press "Test & save". The token stays local.

Requires Ulanzi Studio 3.3.0+ and Home Assistant 2024.1+. Tested on D200X. English and German.

Not affiliated with Home Assistant or the Open Home Foundation.
```
